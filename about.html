About Nebraska Paycheck Calculator

Nebraska Paycheck Calculator is an independent, free educational website that helps workers, job seekers and small employers estimate take-home pay in Nebraska.

What we offer

  • The Nebraska paycheck calculator for salary and hourly estimates, with federal tax, FICA, and Nebraska state withholding
  • An hourly paycheck calculator with overtime
  • Nebraska tax withholding based on Nebraska Circular EN
  • Plain-language Nebraska pay and tax guides

How we build our estimates

Our formulas follow published IRS Publication 15-T percentage-method tables and the Nebraska Department of Revenue Circular EN. We review figures when new tax-year guidance is released.

All calculations run in your browser; we do not store the numbers you enter.

Independence

We are not affiliated with the IRS, the Nebraska Department of Revenue, or any payroll provider. Read our Disclaimer for limits on how estimates should be used.
