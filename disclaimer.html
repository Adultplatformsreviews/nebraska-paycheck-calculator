Disclaimer: Nebraska Paycheck Estimates

The results from our Nebraska paycheck calculator, hourly paycheck calculator, and Nebraska tax withholding tools are estimates for educational purposes only. They are not tax, legal, accounting or payroll advice.

Why your paycheck may differ

  • Employer payroll systems may round or apply tables differently.
  • Pre-tax benefits, retirement contributions, HSAs, garnishments, bonuses and supplemental wage rules may not be fully modeled.
  • Your W-4 and W-4N elections, multiple jobs, or local obligations can change withholding.
  • Tax laws and rates change; figures reflect our best reading of 2027 guidance and may be outdated.

Withholding is not your final tax

Paycheck withholding is a prepayment. Your actual federal and Nebraska income tax is determined when you file your annual returns.

Verify with official sources

Confirm figures with the IRS, the Nebraska Department of Revenue, your employer, or a qualified tax professional.
